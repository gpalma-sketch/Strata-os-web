1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website stores on your device. Spanish law treats other local storage technologies with the same function — such as localStorage — the same way, which is why we cover them here too.

2. What this site uses

This is a static site. It installs no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and embeds no third-party pixels or scripts. All it stores is the following:

NameTypePurposeDuration
strata_cookie_consentFirst-party · technicalRemember your decision about this notice so we do not show it again1 year
strata_langFirst-party · technicalRemember the language you chosePersistent until you clear your browser data

3. Why we do not ask consent for these

Article 22(2) of the LSSI-CE exempts from consent those cookies strictly necessary to provide the service the user requested. Both of the above are: one remembers your own decision about cookies, the other the language you picked. Neither identifies you nor tracks your activity.

Even so, we show the notice so you know exactly what is stored.

4. Analytics

We currently use no analytics tool at all. If we add one in the future, you will be asked for prior consent and it will not load until you give it. This policy will be updated accordingly.

5. How to manage or delete this storage

You can change your decision at any time from the "Cookies" link in the footer, or clear what is stored from your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Third-party services

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may log technical connection data (IP address, user agent) for security and service operation purposes, without installing cookies in your browser. Fonts are served from our own domain, so no request is made to Google Fonts.
